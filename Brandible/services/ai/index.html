A few things you might be wondering.

What is OttoRun?

OttoRun is Brandible's family of business automations. OttoDesk covers reception and scheduling, OttoBlog covers content and publishing, and OttoFollow covers lead follow-up and nurture. All three are available. The mascot is Otto the Otter. The line is: Let OttoRun it.

What problems does it help with?

The ones that steal the day: the phone ringing while you're with a customer, the blog that never gets written, and leads that go cold after a good first conversation.

Where should I start?

Start with one recurring job that pulls you away from your customers or team. Tell us how it works today, and we can talk through which OttoRun workflow fits.

Will it work with the tools I already use?

Tell us which phone, calendar, website, or customer tools are involved. We'll confirm compatibility and any setup requirements for your workflow before you move forward.

How is this different from Brandible's other services?

Brandible still builds websites, brands, and growth systems. OttoRun is the AI automation product line for the work that repeats — calls, content, and follow-ups. You can use OttoRun on its own or alongside other Brandible work.

How much does it cost?

There's no public price list yet. Email ottorun@brandiblemg.com or visit ottorun.brandiblemg.com and tell us which workflow you have in mind. We'll discuss scope and next steps with you.

How do I get started?

Visit ottorun.brandiblemg.com or email ottorun@brandiblemg.com. Tell us about your business and the work you'd like to automate.
